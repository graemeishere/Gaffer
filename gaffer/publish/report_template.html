Chips

A chip is a season-long allocation, not a weekly choice — the question is never "should I play it this week" but "which single week between now and May is its best home". Holding is the default, and stays the default while the weeks that matter are still out of view.

Your mini-league

What decides a league is only the difference between your squad and your rivals'. Points scored on a player everybody owns move the whole table together and change nothing. In a head-to-head league that is even starker: only your one opponent matters that week, margin pays nothing, and a player you both own literally cannot affect the result.
