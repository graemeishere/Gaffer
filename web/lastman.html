How the numbers are built

Win, draw, survive. Every club carries an attack and a defence rating fitted to results, so a fixture's expected goals is attack × opponent defence × home advantage. A pair of goal rates is a distribution over scorelines, and a distribution over scorelines is a win probability. The four lowest scorelines carry the Dixon-Coles correction, because independent Poisson under-counts 0-0 and 1-1 — and in a format where a draw eliminates you, the draw is half the risk rather than a rounding error.

Route. The chance of surviving every round on the plan, not just this one. Eight rounds at 60–70% a time multiplies down to a couple of percent; that is the format, not a fault in the model, and the page prints it rather than hiding it. The number worth watching is the margin over taking the best available team every week, which is quoted on every run.

Field. The one column here that is modelled rather than measured. No pool publishes its picks, so it is a model of where a typical field lands — a softmax over win probability, flattened because everyone else has their own used list. Treat it as a shape, never as evidence.
