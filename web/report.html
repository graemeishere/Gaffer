Your mini-league

What decides a league is only the difference between your squad and your rivals'. Points scored on a player everybody owns move the whole table together and change nothing. In a head-to-head league that is even starker: only your one opponent matters that week, margin pays nothing, and a player you both own literally cannot affect the result.

Cutting Edge Services is a head-to-head league, so one opponent decides each week rather than the whole table. Fixtures are published when the league starts. Once they are, this shows who you are drawn against, your chance of beating them, and whether to take risk on or squeeze it out — which in head-to-head depends on whether you are favourite, not on where you sit in the table.
